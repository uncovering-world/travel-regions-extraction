## abkhazia
- holder: the passage shows Georgia leaving the last part it held (12 August 2008) and names Abkhazian soldiers; the role of Russian forces is in the preceding sentences of the same paragraph, not in the quote. No passage was found on who runs the crossing points (Abkhaz or Russian border guards). UK advice calls it a "Russian occupied region" but the phrase and the name are in separate list items, so it could not be quoted in one paragraph.
- holder_since: 1993 covers "most of Abkhazia" only; the upper Kodori gorge was held by Georgia until August 2008 (in the holder row's passage). The whole area under one holder therefore dates from 12 August 2008.
- stated_outline: Georgia's law names "the territories of the Autonomous Republic of Abkhazia"; the same law mentions "the administrative border at the influx of the Enguri River". Wikipedia: "the Inguri serves as a boundary between Abkhazia and Georgia proper". No Abkhaz-side text was opened. The 1994 ceasefire agreement was annulled in 2008 (Wikipedia), so no ceasefire line is in force.
- contest_ended_by: none; Georgia declared it occupied territory in 2008.

## artsvashen
- holder/holder_since consistent (Azerbaijan, 4 August 1992).
- stated_outline is weak: the passage is the general October 2022 agreement that Soviet-era borders are the basis of delineation; it does not name Artsvashen. No delimitation text for the exclave was found.
- contest_ended_by: none. The page says talks on returning Artsvashen are ongoing.

## chagos
- holder_since: the Chagos Archipelago page says both "ceded ... in 1814" (Treaty of Paris) and "ceded to Britain by treaty only after Napoleon's defeat, in 1815", and that Britain claimed the islands in 1786. 1814 recorded. The BIOT as a separate unit dates from 8 November 1965.
- contest_ended_by: no row. The UK–Mauritius treaty was signed 22 May 2025 but is not ratified; ratification was suspended in 2026 (BIOT page: "Following backlash from United States President Donald Trump in 2026, ratification has been suspended"). If it enters into force it is the holder ceding sovereignty, not the claimant giving up.
- stated_outline: islands, no line on land; the row records that the territory is the seven atolls. Possible mis-scope: the register also has an area `diego-garcia` (seen in an earlier batch); under the treaty Diego Garcia would stay under UK control, so the two areas would diverge only on ratification.

## donetsk-occupied
- holder: latest share found is "around 55% by June 2022"; no later sourced figure in the pages read.
- holder_since: the passage is a map caption ("parts of ... Donetsk Oblast (6) since 2014"). The held part has grown and shrunk since; there is no single date for the whole held part.
- stated_outline: `none: the line is moving`, supported by a settlement-table entry (Kostiantynivka "Contested by Russia since 4 November 2025"). Russia claims the whole oblast (annexation of 30 September 2022), i.e. the claim's line is the oblast border, but the held part is bounded by the front.
- Scope: the area is "whatever part Russia holds", so its extent changes with the front.

## gazakh-exclaves
- holder: the passage (Armenia–Azerbaijan border page) is in a section tagged "does not cite any sources"; it also lists Karki, which is a separate area. Barxudarlı's "under the control of Armenia ever since" sentence carries a citation-needed tag.
- holder_since: exact date only for the Barxudarlı–Sofulu exclave (27 April 1992). Yukhari Askipara: "occupied by Armenia since 1992" (no day). Sofulu page says only "since the First Nagorno-Karabakh War".
- stated_outline: weak, same generic passage as artsvashen.
- contest_ended_by: none. Yukhari Askipara page: Armenia's prime minister in 2024 "publicly reiterated Azerbaijan's sovereignty over the seven villages" — the holder acknowledging the claimant, not an end of the contest; the villages have not been handed over.
- Scope: the area bundles two separate exclaves (Yukhari Askipara; Barxudarlı+Sofulu). Four non-exclave villages of the same district (Aşağı Əskipara, Bağanis Ayrum, Qızılhacılı, Xeyrimli) were returned to Azerbaijan on 24 May 2024 and must not be counted in it.

## golan-heights
- holder_since 1967 holds for the part west of the 1974 line. Israel captured the whole Heights including Quneitra in June 1967 and handed about a third back under the 1974 agreement.
- stated_outline: the Purple Line / UNDOF "Alpha" line. Since 8 December 2024 Israel also holds the UN buffer zone east of that line ("Israel occupied the demilitarized buffer zone in the Golan Heights as a 'temporary defensive position'", plus two Syrian villages), and Israel cited "the collapse of the 1974 ceasefire agreement". So the stated line no longer bounds what Israel holds; the buffer zone and beyond may need a separate area (holder Israel, since December 2024).
- contest_ended_by: none ("Syria and Israel still contest the ownership of the Heights").

## israeli-posts-south-lebanon
- Mis-scoped as named. The id refers to the five border positions Israel kept after 18 February 2025; since the ground invasion of 16 March 2026 the occupied area is 570–600 km2, and Israel declared a "yellow line" zone of 55 towns and villages. The register's own `parties` fact already describes the 2026 occupation.
- holder_since: the row gives 18 February 2025 for the five positions only. The Wikipedia footnote says "some 10 further square kilometers ... since 2024" (i.e. continuous from the October 2024 invasion). The wider zone dates from March 2026. No single date fits the whole area.
- stated_outline: the "yellow line" is stated by Israel only (date "on the same day" is not in the quoted paragraph; the cited news items are dated 17–18 April 2026). The same page says the IDF "advanced beyond the 'yellow line'" on 24 August, so `none: the line is moving` is a defensible alternative. Lebanon's stated limit (the Blue Line) was not sourced.
- UK advice (primary): "fighting and airstrikes have continued particularly in southern Lebanon, where an Israeli military presence remains" — not used, it does not say who controls entry.
- Shebaa Farms (held since 1967) is a different area.

## kinmen-matsu
- holder_since: sourced for Kinmen only (3 October 1945, after the Japanese occupation). For Matsu the only passage found says the KMT "retained" the islands when it retreated at the end of 1949; no start date. The PLA landing on Kinmen on 25 October 1949 was repelled, so the hold was not interrupted.
- stated_outline: no row. No line stated by the parties was found; Dadeng, Xiaodeng and Jiaoyu (claimed by the ROC as part of Kinmen County) have been under PRC control since October 1949, so the held area is not the whole county.
- contest_ended_by: none.
- Scope: two separate island groups in one area.

## kosovo
- holder: UK advice says Kosovo's authorities set and enforce entry rules. North Kosovo ran Serbia-backed parallel institutions until the 2013 Brussels Agreement (North Kosovo page), so for the north the holder date is later than for the rest.
- holder_since: the row gives 10 June 1999, the start of UN administration after Yugoslav forces withdrew — the date from which Serbia has not held it. Kosovo's own institutions as holder date from the declaration of 17 February 2008 (both dates are in the quoted infobox line). Which of the two the settling rule wants is a model choice.
- contest_ended_by: no row. Serbia "accepts the governing authority of the Kosovo institutions as part of the 2013 Brussels Agreement" but "continues to claim" Kosovo; that is not a renunciation.
- stated_outline: taken from the claim (Serbia's Autonomous Province of Kosovo and Metohija); the passage names the unit, not a line. UK advice: Serbia does "not consider the designated crossing points with Kosovo to be official international border crossing points".

## luhansk-occupied
- stated_outline: weakest of the Ukrainian three. Russia claims the entire oblast and Russian-backed officials say they hold all of it (July 2025, again April 2026); Ukraine denies it; the page says fighting "continues in some places". Recorded `none: the line is moving`; if the oblast is in fact fully held, the stated outline would be the oblast border (LPR page: Russia's recognition was "based on constitutional claims to the entire Luhansk region as it existed within Ukraine").
- holder_since: 2014 for "large parts ... including the capital Luhansk"; the rest from 2022.

## somaliland
- holder: split. Somaliland holds the west and centre; after the Las Anod conflict a "significant portion of its eastern territory" is held by SSC-Khatumo (a footnote gives about 152,483 km2 controlled of 177,125 km2 claimed). So the area as one unit with one holder is mis-scoped: its eastern part has a different holder since 2023 (Somaliland army withdrew from around Las Anod in August 2023).
- holder_since: 1991 is the declaration of independence, not a dated statement of control; the day (18 May 1991) is in the infobox only.
- stated_outline: no row. No passage states the claimed border as a line; the page says only that Somaliland "regards itself as the successor state to British Somaliland".
- UK advice (primary), not used as a row: a Somali eVisa "will not be considered valid for entry when you arrive in Hargeisa" — Somalia's entry rules are not enforced there.
- contest_ended_by: none.

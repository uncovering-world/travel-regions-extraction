## Self-check

`selfcheck6.py --refetch`, run after the last change: **191 rows checked, 0 failing, 0 dropped; 71 distinct sources, all re-opened.**

- A (the required check): the quote, whitespace-normalised, is a substring of the saved page text of its `source_url`.
- B: the quote is found the way `data/disputed-areas/verify_quotes.py` looks for it (its `squash()`), on the text cached when the page was fetched.
- C: every `source_url` was opened again from scratch exactly as `verify_quotes.page_text()` does (Wikipedia by `oldid`, other pages with the project's user agent) and B was repeated on the fresh text.
- D: header, allowed values, numeric area, one row per (area, field), area in the batch, quote of at least 15 characters, no line break, ellipsis or reference marker in a quote.

Following the finding in the batch 4 notes (the helper strips `[ 12 ]` markers, the verifier does not), quotes were cut on text with the markers kept, so no quote runs across a marker. That is why some quotes start or end in mid-sentence. During the build no row was rejected for a missing passage; the two build failures were my own anchor syntax and were fixed before any output was kept.

## General limits

- `parties`: the value was trimmed to what the row's own quote says. Roles that a different passage carries are in `on_the_ground`, `kind` or `origin` of the same area. One exception: for `ta-muen-thom-emerald-triangle` the quoted sentence does not name the two states; they are the subject of the article ("Cambodian–Thai border dispute").
- `origin`: every quote carries its year; for the three `resolved_recently` areas the settlement date is in the quote. `three-pagodas-pass` has no `origin`.
- `traveller_access` is sourced for 13 areas only. For `south-korea-claimed-by-dprk`, `sutorina` and `taiwan` it is the country's general entry rule for British passport holders, not a rule about the area as such. FCDO "advises against travel" statements were not turned into values.
- `area_km2` values that are not a plain reading of one figure: Spratly 2 ("less than 200 ha" of natural land, an upper bound); Tin Bigha 0.01513 (computed from "178 by 85 metres"); Tromelin 0.8 (80 ha); Tunbs 12.3 (10.3 + 2, both in one infobox line); Wadi Halfa 210 (whole salient, of which only 30–40 km² is land today); Taiwan 36193 (all territory under ROC control, see Taiwan); Susta 50 (infobox; the text speaks of 14,860 ha).
- Infobox fragments (short, but verbatim): Susta `area_km2`, Transnistria `area_km2`, Wake `area_km2`, Tunbs `area_km2`, Tiran `inhabited` ("Population 0 (2026)"), Ta Muen Thom `on_the_ground`.
- Rows quoted from "List of territorial disputes" are table cells: South Korea `parties`, `origin`, `on_the_ground`; Strovilia `on_the_ground`; Sudan–South Sudan `parties`; Swains `kind`.
- UPDATING.md now lists ten kinds (the task said nine); `unclaimed` is not used in this batch.

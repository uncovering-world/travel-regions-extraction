Which single practical difference is being checked? Define disjoint scopes of comparison, the date and the class of civilian short-stay visit. Do not assume the whole state is homogeneous.

## What the sources say

Keep each statement separate and link it to a specific section. State who publishes it: the competent local authority, the text of a law, a guide, foreign travel advice. Record the date of reading separately from the publication date and from the date the rule takes effect. Keep a short excerpt; say explicitly when no full archived copy exists.

## Who is affected and what exceptions exist

Nationality, passport, visa/permit, residence, family rights, purpose, duration and the relevant route. Do not fill an unknown parameter with an assumption. Distinguish the validity of a document from guaranteed admission.

## Conclusion and limits

What does the text support directly? What is research interpretation? What is missing in facts, and what in the general definition? Do not turn a source that was read into a fully verified proof, or the absence of proof into equality.

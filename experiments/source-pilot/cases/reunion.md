# Réunion and European France: exceptions change the answer

## Question

Is an ordinary Schengen visa enough for both territories? The name of the state does not answer this, and the word "overseas" does not by itself create a boundary either.

## What was read

The official French public-service guide explains a separate visa procedure for the overseas territories, Réunion included, and exemptions based on residence permits, type D visas, nationality and document type. It points to checking the specific situation and to territorial regulations. [Service Public](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10610).

France-Visas describes ordinary Schengen and overseas visas separately. Beyond the general exemptions, it mentions special cases for nationals of certain countries holding a long-validity French multiple-entry visa. So the statement "no Schengen visa ever helps for Réunion" would be too strong. [France-Visas](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/visa-de-court-sejour).

## What could not be checked

The previously used page on France in the Schengen area did not load. The link from Service Public to the territorial regulation returned an access denial. We read an alternative official page but **do not treat it as a substitute for the full list of exceptions**. Failed retrievals are recorded in the [source register](../sources.json).

## Conclusion and limits

There is a source-supported mechanism for a difference in visa coverage. However, this pilot did not establish a concrete class "nationality × passport × visa × no exemptions" for which both sides are fully checked on one date.

Candidate: a short tourist trip by a non-European national with an ordinary short-stay type C visa, without a long-term multiple-entry visa or residence grounds. The nationality is deliberately left empty for now: filling in a convenient name without checking the list of exceptions would invent a missing fact.

Next step: obtain the applicable text of the regulation, or a reproducible result from the official visa assistant for a concrete class; if the assistant is used, record every parameter entered. Until then we do not create a finished proof of separation.

The product requirement to keep Réunion separate remains accepted, but it is not used as proof of a visa difference. The gap does not mean Réunion should be merged with European France, and it is not treated as solved by a future destination stage.

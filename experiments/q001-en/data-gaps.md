# Data gaps

## Missing factual evidence

- **C010 Bermuda:** the nationality schedule to the MRV Order has not been extracted; the 45-day rule is established, but a concrete passport witness is not completed. The final admission delegation also requires the law.
- **C011 Gibraltar:** the visa changes and airport arrangements have been read; the independence of the veto and the exact current nationality matrix require the treaty/implementing law.
- **C016–C025:** the general destination-visa distinctions are established. For a strict positive R007 witness a full nationality × passport × endorsement exception lookup is missing. For Guadeloupe/Martinique mutual visa validity is not assumed.
- **French overseas, Dutch countries:** local admission office, constitutional autonomy and final decision independence are not equated. The current delegating norms and the powers to refuse/cancel stay are needed. Bonaire KMar/IND-CN does not mean a separate municipal admission jurisdiction.
- **Ordinary controls:** national competence and general visa guidance are limited evidence; exhaustive equality for all documents, routes and permits is not proven. The Sicily constitutional source is provisional; the Spain ordinary-region final authority requires additional normative confirmation.
- **Western Sahara:** not established: uniform tourist admission rules for east/south, the current permitted routes Algeria→east and Mauritania→east, the exact checkpoint procedures in the west. The route table distinguishes the unknown from the closed. The Morocco–Algeria closure must not be carried over to other sections of the border. A planned road is not counted as an open border crossing.
- **CIP:** Aksai Chin and Shaksgam — reliable ordinary civilian route/permit/admission data are absent. Jammu and Kashmir / Ladakh / GB / AJK — an incomplete current civil-authority decomposition, claimant/international-status dossier and chain of final border authority. The Siachen base camp does not replace a study of the glacier area. For the Line of Actual Control no uniform civilian regime is established.
- **Refugee/emergency documents:** the Caribbean I-571 exemption has been checked; the pairs for emergency passports and individual Schengen recognition differences are not yet closed. Do not extend ordinary passport findings to these categories.

## Conflicting / non-equivalent source statements

- S13–S14 contain an outdated list of Schengen states: do not use it as a current authoritative full list. The description of Portugal airport procedures by itself does not prove an independent jurisdiction of the islands.
- S44 (2019 NOC waiver) and S42–S43 (registration / district permissions) look contradictory only when time and scope are lost. Waiver, registration and sensitive-area permission are different requirements; a universal "NOC is not needed" is not recorded.
- S25 inaccurately groups part of the overseas in an informational heading: constitutional status is not extracted from it.
- The claims of the parties in S35/S47 are not resolved by a vote of sources. The claim and the observations of control are stored separately.

## Uncertain geometry

- For Western Sahara there is no exact current civil/military/border polygon. The UN 2025 snapshot and the FCDO coarse control do not justify an exact 2026 line. The Berm danger/advice buffers are not considered legally restricted polygons.
- LoC and LAC affected areas are overlays with undefined boundaries; the protected-area schedule and the claimed international boundary do not replace actual control.
- Siachen Glacier is not equal to the tourist base camp. Aksai Chin, Shaksgam and eastern Western Sahara do not receive administrative polygons from claim maps.
- Reference scopes and some pairs overlap intentionally: status object/zone, region/overlay. They must not be fed to the evaluator as geographic partitions known to be disjoint.

## Temporal uncertainty

- **Faroe/Greenland:** a new residence-document exemption from 20 March 2026. The old Schengen-residence counterexample is unusable (E17).
- **Gibraltar:** effective 15 July 2026; do not apply the former UK visa/residence waiver (E24–E25).
- **Ladakh:** the 24 August 2026 all-UT PAP is a proposal; in the dataset it does not become a current obligation (E43).
- The S35 control observations are September 2025; AP Aksai/Arunachal — August 2023; the Siachen military report — April 2024. They are not relabelled as current confirmed polygons.
- An outdated block about the June 2026 elections was found in S42; the freshness of the registration rule is marked qualified. The UNMOGIP deployment excerpt does not date the whole control situation.
- The EUR-Lex full-text is unavailable; the C001 LTV witness is provisional until the applicable version is confirmed. In the main question it has not been upgraded to true.

## Genuine model questions

- **Q004:** which customs/VAT/biosecurity difference is material for R007? A tax territorial scope without a traveler witness does not give P1=true.
- **Q005/Q009:** how to take destination permits and spatial overlays into account without turning every restricted section into a region? The Arunachal whole-territory PAP and the Ladakh partial PAP give different scope structures.
- **Q001.identity/R010:** the general identity predicate is not defined; therefore all `territorial_identity_candidate` are left unresolved. **Q006** applies only to the additional question of whether a disputed/occupied/international-status identity should be preserved without a proven travel discontinuity.
- **Q007:** should civil, military, border and enforcement control be compared separately; which composition is needed for P2? West/east Western Sahara does not answer this automatically.
- **Q008/R009:** how to work with time and the incompleteness of contexts? Unknown is not a proven equivalence and not a hard split.

Additional search can close the factual gaps, but does not choose the answers to these model questions.

## au-ashmore-cartier
- The Wikipedia lead (Visa policy of Australia) is about a **visa exemption**: Migration Regulations 1994 reg 2.40(1)(t) gives a special purpose visa to Indonesian traditional fishermen visiting the Territory. It relaxes the rules and does not restrict who may come. Read from an AustLII consolidation archived 2024-08-18 (web.archive.org). The live AustLII and legislation.gov.au copies returned 403 or could not be fetched, so the current wording is **not confirmed for 2026**.
- The actual access restriction is **by zone, not for the whole territory**: approval from the Director of National Parks is needed for vessels to enter the Ashmore Reef Sanctuary Zone, and express prior written approval is needed to land on Cartier Island (Parks Australia pages, live). The Recreational Use Zone, including a small area of West Island, is open to visitors. The older departmental page (archived 2026-09-08) says East and Middle Islands are "by permit only".
- **Not found:** the instrument that imposes the approval requirement (presumably the North-west Marine Parks Network Management Plan 2018 under the EPBC Act; unchecked) and its start date. So `start_date` is left out.
- `unit_level=top_level` refers to the external territory. The rule's own area (the zones) is smaller than the unit.
- The infrastructure.gov.au live site refused connections, so archived snapshots were used.

## au-macquarie-island
- The rule is written for the island itself: reg 18(2)(b) of the National Parks and Reserves Management Regulations 2019 (Tas) names "Macquarie Island Nature Reserve". The regulations took effect on 2019-12-21. Restricted since 1979 (Management Plan 2006).
- `unit_level=not_a_unit`: the island is part of Tasmania and lies inside Huon Valley Municipality (Management Plan 2006). It is not an administrative unit of its own. This is a judgement on that quote. If the owner treats it as `lower`, the source still holds.
- Wikipedia's "written authorisation of the Director of National Parks and Wildlife" matches the 2006 plan. The current guidelines call it an "Access Authority issued by PWS".
- Second instrument: under Commonwealth migration law (reg 2.40(1)(p), (12)), persons visiting Macquarie Island hold a special purpose visa only while on the island and only with advance written permission from the Tasmanian Secretary. Taken from the 2024 archived consolidation, **not confirmed as current**.
- `changed_last_10y=unclear`: the instrument was remade in 2019. Whether the requirement itself changed was not checked.
- The Management Plan 2006 PDF is 13.8 MB. The first download was truncated, so it was downloaded again in full.

## mh-kwajalein
- Instrument: 32 CFR Part 525, "Entry Authorization Regulation for Kwajalein Missile Range". Read from the official eCFR point-in-time text for 2026-09-01. Source: 48 FR 34028, 1983-07-27. Entry control passed to the Army in July 1964.
- **Issuer:** the National Range Commander, who handles "all other personnel", i.e. ordinary visitors. Official, resident and RMI categories go to the Commander, KMR. **Coverage:** only the US defense sites ("KMR"). The text states that the authorizations do not apply to any other area of the Marshall Islands. Ebeye and other RMI-administered islands are outside KMR. Transit through KMR to RMI islands needs an RMI Chief of Immigration permit.
- **Not established:** the "Entry Authorization … obtained on arrival" for visitors to Ebeye (Timatic, via Wikipedia). It is probably a transit authorization at Bucholz Army Airfield (unchecked). No primary USAG-KA page on visitor entry could be fetched: the army.mil articles returned stubs and home.army.mil gave an unrelated PDF.
- The CFR text still uses the old names (KMR, BMDSCOM). `in_force=yes` rests only on the part still being in the 2026-09-01 CFR. Current administrative practice is unverified.
- `detached=island` rests on Wikipedia (11 of 97 islands leased), which is secondary.
- Kwajalein Atoll itself is an ISO 3166-2 municipality, MH-KWA (Wikipedia ISO 3166-2:MH, pinned). ISO lists two levels: chains and municipalities. Whether MH-KWA counts as top-level was not decided here.

## in-punjab-kerala-scheme-exclusion
- Scheme: Visa on Arrival at Wagha/Attari for Pakistani passport holders over 65 who come to meet friends or relatives. It runs under the India-Pakistan Visa Agreement 2012 and is available from 2013-03-31. The exclusion text comes from the High Commission of India, Islamabad: the live page, last updated 2026-09-16, and a 2023-03-19 archive. That text also excludes **Jammu and Kashmir**, which the Wikipedia passage omits.
- The **MHA manual** (Annex V, 2018 and 2020-08-13) gives the Visa-on-Arrival terms (45 days, up to 5 places, Attari only, on foot) but **does not state the Punjab/Kerala exclusion**. No Bureau of Immigration text was found.
- `in_force=no`: on 2025-04-24 India suspended visa services to Pakistani nationals, and all existing visas were revoked from 2025-04-27 (High Commission press page, live). **No restoration notice was found, but that absence was not proven.** The Visa-on-Arrival page itself is still online and does not mention the suspension.
- Punjab and Kerala are rated `top_level` from pinned Wikipedia pages (secondary). `detached=no` rests on a weak secondary quote.
- This is an exclusion from a facilitation scheme, not an entry rule for those states. Census `regime_kind=visa_exemption` describes the scheme.

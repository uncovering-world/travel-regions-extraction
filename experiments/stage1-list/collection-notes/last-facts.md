# facts4: notes

Read on 2026-10-03. 15 rows: 9 in `rules-out.csv` and 6 in `register-out.csv`. They cover 13 of the 22 asked facts. Mount Athos and Tristan each have two `start_date` rows, which give the two steps of the legal basis.

## How the search was done, and its limit

- The session's WebSearch budget (200 calls) was already used up when this run began, so no WebSearch call was possible. No general search engine was scraped to get around the limit. One test request to bing.com was sent; it returned no usable results, and nothing from it was used.
- Leads came from direct fetches of known addresses instead: Wikipedia and Wikivoyage pages pinned by `oldid` (`wp.py`, `voy.py`), the `externallinks` of Wikipedia pages, the Wayback CDX index, and the search boxes of single sites (Wikipedia's own search, azernews.az, gobiernogalapagos.gob.ec). Each source's text is saved in `pages/`. `manifest.tsv` maps each cited URL to its saved text.
- `get.py` saves HTML as text. HTML source newlines are turned into spaces, because they are not paragraph breaks. The fix came in midway; only the Azernews page was fetched again with it (`az_travel_161925_v2.txt`).
- Self-check: `python3 selfcheck.py` found 15 of 15 quotes as verbatim substrings of their saved text, each on one line, at least 15 characters, with no citation marker, and every Wikipedia URL pinned. Nothing was dropped. The Camp David quote contains no-break spaces (U+00A0) exactly as on the page.

## Weakest rows

- `az-nakhchivan / in_force`: the newest statement found dates from **2020-02-18** (Azernews, quoting Azerbaijan's tourism agency). Nothing from 2025–2026 was found. Earlier collectors noted that gov.uk says the Iran–Azerbaijan land borders are "temporarily closed"; that context still holds.
- `iq-kurdistan / start_date = 2023-03-01`: the Wikipedia sentence dates the start of the current rule, a visa for every traveller with a KRG e-Visa or visa on arrival. It does not say when the Region's own visa first began. That page also says that since September 2025 the federal e-Visa portal is "solely responsible", so the earlier contradiction is still open.
- `my-labuan / start_date = 2022-11-01`: Bernama (via Astro Awani, 2022-12-06) says this is when immigration checks began at the Labuan ferry terminal for travellers leaving for mainland Sabah. The checks were described as *stricter* control after abuse of a "dasar aliran bebas" (free-flow policy). This is Sabah-bound control at Labuan's exit, so whether it is Labuan's own rule or Sabah's rule applied at Labuan is a modelling question. `in_force` rests on Wikipedia (Malaysian identity card): Peninsular Malaysians arriving in Labuan obtain an IMM.114. No imi.gov.my page that names Labuan was found.
- `tort-kocho-road / inhabited = no`: rests on a 2022 statement of intent by President Japarov, quoted by 24.kg in 2025: houses "will be removed in the center". No source confirms the removal was carried out.
- `kafia-kingi / holder_since = 1960`: the Rift Valley Institute (2010) says the enclave was transferred to Darfur in 1960 and is "currently under the administration of South Darfur". The Wikipedia article says South Sudanese forces "have briefly controlled large portions" at times, without a date. An unbroken hold since 1960 is therefore not proven. The other candidate, "controlled ever since then [2005] by Sudanese forces" (South Sudan–Sudan relations, oldid 1377684449), says "largely".
- `armistice-no-mans-lands / kind = paper_claim`: the passage covers the **Latrun** No Man's Land only. Israel's definition of the West Bank leaves it out, and the Palestinian definition includes it. The value is a choice: Israel administers the area as ordinary territory, and the Palestinian side claims it as part of the West Bank. If a source showing that the area is widely regarded as occupied is found, `occupied_or_annexed` would replace it. The Jerusalem no-man's-land and Mount Scopus are not covered.
- `ilemi-triangle / inhabited = yes`: rests on the Wikipedia stub "Lokomarinyang", a village it places inside the Ilemi Triangle. Its infobox says "Administered by South Sudan", which conflicts with the register's statement that Kenya controls the whole triangle de facto.
- `gr-mount-athos / start_date = 1927`: this is when the current legal basis took effect. Article 176 of the Charter of Mount Athos was ratified by Legislative Decree of 10/16 September 1926 (ΦΕΚ Α΄ 309/16.9.1926) and, according to the page, came into force in 1927. A permit custom may be older, but no source read dates one.
- `ec-galapagos / start_date = 1998`: this dates the special migration regime (Ley Especial de Galápagos), not the TCT card. A CGREG press release of 2023-11-17 says the card is governed by the LOREG and "la Ordenanza 001 del año 2017". That is the current regulatory basis, not a start date, so no row was written for it.
- `sh-tristan-da-cunha`: these rows are primary, from the official revised edition on sainthelena.gov.sh. Permission to land comes from the Entry Control (Landing) Regulations, 1989, whose day is not stated. The parent Ordinance has been in force since 14 August 1967.

## Not found (no row)

- **cn-tibet / start_date**: read Tourism in Tibet (en), 西藏旅游 (zh), Internal border control (en), Tibet Autonomous Region (en), Wikivoyage Tibet, three travel-agency guides (windhorsetour, itibettravel, tibetctrip), and the archived TAR government page "入藏函" (xizang.gov.cn, 2016 snapshot). None dates the permit. The only date is "Foreign tourists were first permitted to visit in the 1980s".
- **in-minicoy-maldivians / start_date**: the only source on Wikipedia (cruisingmaldives.blog) is now a private WordPress site. The archived Indian High Commission in Malé visa pages (FAQ, guidelines, general; 2025 snapshots) do not mention Minicoy. hcimale.gov.in returns 403 directly.
- **tj-gbao / start_date**: read Gorno-Badakhshan (en, ru), Visa policy of Tajikistan (en, ru), M41 highway, and Wikivoyage Pamirs. All describe the permit; none dates it. The legal act behind it (a border-zone regulation) was not located without a search engine.
- **moldauhafen / traveller_access**: read Moldauhafen and Hamburg-Kleiner Grasbrook (de). Neither says anything about public access.
- **russian-ranges-kazakhstan / traveller_access**: Сары-Шаган (полигон) (ru) says the range "перестала охраняться … фактически доступным для всех желающих", while official permission took a long time. Приозёрск (ru) says entry to the town has been without passes since 2005. Neither describes the part leased to Russia today.
- **suleyman-shah-tomb / traveller_access**: Turkish and English Wikipedia describe only the pre-2015 site. Hürriyet Daily News of 2015-02-24 carries the defence minister's invitation to visit the new site near Ashme. That is a 2015 intention, not an access rule, so no row.
- **tiwinza / traveller_access**: nothing beyond what earlier collectors found ("Tiwinza (Perú)", es, oldid 169256370: the area has not yet been demined).
- **doi-lang / parties, kind**: searched Thai Wikipedia (its own search for "ดอยลาง"; the pages for the Mae Ai district, Mae Ai subdistrict, Doi Pha Hom Pok National Park and Thailand–Myanmar relations) and the CIA Factbook disputes for Thailand and Burma (pinned mirror commit 588c5b5). Nothing mentions a Doi Lang dispute. In "List of territorial disputes" the Doi Lang row has an empty description, with Myanmar in bold (the controlling party by that table's convention). That is not a quotable passage.

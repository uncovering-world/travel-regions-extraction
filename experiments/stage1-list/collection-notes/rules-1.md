# rules-out-1 — notes

Read on 2026-10-03. 140 rows for 22 rules; 10 rules have all seven fields, none has zero.
Working files: `r1/` (`manifest.tsv` maps each `source_url` to the saved text in `r1/pages/`; `rows.py` + `build.py` cut each quote out of the saved text; `selfcheck.py` re-checks it).

Conventions and limits

- `primary` = official host only (bcn.cl, msgg.gob.cl, eur-lex and legifrance / outre-mer.gouv.fr through web.archive.org snapshots, lakshadweep.gov.in document store, boi.gov.in, ilp.nagaland.gov.in, visit.gov.krd, korea.net, imi.gov.my, чукотка.рф, sainthelena.gov.sh, lovdata.no, mfa.tj, immigration.gov.tw, tzembassy.go.tz). Copies of official texts on other hosts (Colombian decree on ligacontraelsilencio.com, Russian decree on consultant.ru / legalacts.ru) are marked `secondary`.
- legifrance.gouv.fr, outre-mer.gouv.fr and eur-lex refused direct download; the rows cite the web.archive.org snapshot actually read (legifrance 2024-11-09, outre-mer 2026-08-18, eur-lex 2024-11-18).
- mfa.tj was fetched with certificate verification off (its chain did not validate).
- Two rules carry two rows for one field: `co-san-andres/what_it_requires` (card; stay limit) and `fr-clipperton/start_date` (entry-into-force clause; publication date).
- `in_force = yes` from a Wikipedia sentence in the present tense, or from an official page that is merely still online, is weak; such rows are named below.
- The web-search budget of the session ran out midway, so several official texts were not located (listed per row).

Per rule

- **az-nakhchivan** — Only source for the rule is the news item (Azernews, 2019-11-28), quoting Iran's consulate. No official Azerbaijani text found. `in_force` not established: the pinned "Visa policy of Azerbaijan" page does not mention Nakhchivan at all. Lead confirmed otherwise (15 days, from 2019-12-01, visa for the rest of Azerbaijan obtainable at Nakhchivan airport).
- **cl-rapa-nui** — Law 21.070 read in the BCN consolidated text. Applies to "toda persona, chilena o extranjera"; 30 days. Unit level `lower` (a province and commune of Valparaíso Region; constitutionally a "special territory") — Wikipedia. The lead's "entry form, return ticket and accommodation" was not checked against the law's articles.
- **co-san-andres** — Decree 2762 of 1991 read in a PDF copy on a non-official host (funcionpublica.gov.co and suin-juriscol.gov.co did not respond). Art. 14–17 confirm the tourist card and the four-month limit. `in_force` rests on a 2024 news item (Infobae) about the card's price; no 2026 source. The lead's claim that Colombians are included is consistent with "Los que deseen visitar ... en calidad de turistas" but the text does not say it in so many words.
- **es-ceuta** — Contradiction with the lead's framing: the declaration is one rule for **Ceuta and Melilla** together (`own_rule = list`), and the visa exemption is for local border traffic with Tetuan **and Nador** provinces. Not a general independent-visitor rule: it exempts residents of the neighbouring Moroccan provinces; for everyone else the territory-specific element is the document check on departure. `start_date` not established (the text read gives no signature date for the accession agreement). `in_force` only from Wikipedia. Looks marginal for scope.
- **fr-clipperton** — Contradiction with the lead: authorisation is issued by the **minister in charge of overseas territories**, not the High Commissioner in French Polynesia (Wikipedia: administration moved to the minister on 2007-02-21). Decree 2023-1182 published in the JORF of 2023-12-15, in force the next day. The lead's "2008" was not checked; the arrêté of 2025-11-03 is mentioned on the ministry page but its text was not read. `not_a_unit` (state private property under the minister) rests on Wikipedia. Uninhabited — landing authorisation rather than an immigration rule.
- **in-lakshadweep** — 1967 Rules read on the administration's site: permit for every non-native; in force 1967-10-01. Contradiction with the lead: the Bureau of Immigration list of Restricted/Protected areas read today does **not** name Lakshadweep, and Wikipedia lists "All of Lakshadweep" under *formerly* protected or restricted areas; the rules themselves speak of "Foreign nationals permitted by the Ministry of Home Affairs". The lead's restriction of foreigners to Agatti, Bangaram and Kadmat was not found in any text read. `in_force` rests on the administration's entry-permit page being current (it lists issuing authorities, including "Foreigners — Administrator"). The PDF text has margin notes interleaved, so the quotes are short clean fragments.
- **in-minicoy-maldivians** — Single Wikipedia sentence ("Visa policy of India"); no official text found, no start date. `lower` (a census town in Lakshadweep). Applies to Maldivian citizens only. Weak throughout.
- **in-nagaland** — Two rules. (a) Protected Area Permit for foreigners: Nagaland is one line in a list (`own_rule = list`, Bureau of Immigration). (b) Inner Line Permit, prescribed by the Government of Nagaland, for "anyone, both Indian and Foreigner" not indigenous — the state's own regime, in force "since 1873" per the state portal. Contradiction with the lead's "1958" as the only date; 1958 is the Protected Areas Order (Wikipedia only). The lead's "relaxations" for foreigners were not found in the texts read; Wikipedia says Nagaland was excluded from the PAP regime 2010-12-30 to 2012-12-31, and the official list today names "Whole of Nagaland". Wikipedia's general PAP conditions ("groups of at least 2", "registered travel agent") would matter for the independent-visitor test but were not confirmed in an official text.
- **iq-kurdistan** — Contradiction between sources: the KRG e-visa portal is online today ("© 2026 - KRG", offers applications, 30-day stay), while Wikipedia says "As of September 2025, the Iraqi federal government's 'e-Visa Portal' is solely responsible for providing visas" and elsewhere that "Iraqi Kurdistan has its own eVisa platform". `in_force = yes` rests only on the portal being live — treat as unresolved. Nothing read says the KRG visa is invalid outside the Region. No start date.
- **ir-qeshm** — Wikipedia only. `own_rule = list`: the 14-day visa-free stay is for "Kish Island, Qeshm Island, or other free trade zones" (seven zones named). `lower` (part of Qeshm County); the page names the province as "Daraghan", which looks like an error in that revision — the quote was cut before it. `in_force` rests on a sentence dated 2022. No official Iranian text found.
- **kr-jeju** — Korea.net (government site, September 2025) confirms 30-day visa-free entry "for both individual and group tourists". The Special Act article was not read; no start date established (a search result mentioned 2002-05-01 — unverified). The nationality exclusions and K-ETA / direct-flight conditions are from Wikipedia.
- **mx-southern-border-tvr** — Weakest rule. gob.mx returned an empty page, the archive refused it, diputados.gob.mx timed out. Only passage: Wikipedia "Visa requirements for Guatemalan citizens": 7 days with TVR, "allows entry into the **four** Southern States" — contradicts the lead's five states. The states, the other nationalities, start date and current status are not established. The scope is a group of states, not one unit (`not_a_unit`).
- **my-sabah** — Immigration Department pages: section 66 restricts citizens' entry into "the State of East Malaysia (Sabah / Sarawak)" — one rule for both states (`own_rule = list`), each top-level. West Malaysians get a visit pass of up to three months on arrival. The Act's own text (Part VII) was not obtained (commonlii and refworld refused); the 1963 date is from Wikipedia's infobox. The rule read concerns Malaysian citizens; nothing was read about separate permission for foreign visitors arriving from the Peninsula. `detached = no`: Sabah is on Borneo with a land border to Sarawak; it is separated from Peninsular Malaysia by sea but is neither an island nor an exclave in the strict sense.
- **ru-chukotka** — Contradictions with the lead: (1) the whole okrug is one item (no. 17) in the federal list of territories with regulated visiting (`own_rule = list`); (2) it was **added on 1997-12-27** (Decree 1641), not in 1992. Decree text from consultant.ru / legalacts.ru (edition of 2019-12-20), not from the official publication portal. The okrug's page says the permit is obtained by a **host organization** inviting the foreigner — matters for the independent-visitor test.
- **ru-former-regional-evisas** — Wikipedia only. Regional e-visas from 2017, country-wide from 2020 (`in_force = no`). The lead's "2021" / named regions (Kaliningrad, St Petersburg) were not quoted; only the Far Eastern passage was. A set of federal subjects, not one place — `unit_level` and `detached` left out. Out of scope as a current rule.
- **sh-saint-helena** — Government immigration pages: entry permit for everyone without St Helenian status; short-term permit up to 183 days; separate e-visa for some passports. No start date. The lead's "long list of visa-free nationalities" was not quoted (the checker page is interactive). St Helena as one of three constituent parts of the territory: Wikipedia.
- **sj-jan-mayen** — Regulations of 1962 on foreigners' access to Jan Mayen (Lovdata). Differs from the lead: no blanket prior permission — the police may permit a stay of up to one week on arrival; a longer stay needs prior permission of the Ministry of Justice. §§ 1–7 (passport, visa, entry and exit control) were repealed from 2022-05-01. `not_a_unit`: administered by the County Governor of Nordland (Wikipedia). Nothing read on Svalbard.
- **tj-gbao** — MFA (Vienna embassy page): special entry permission for foreign nationals and stateless persons, 20 euros, valid for six named districts. No start date. evisa.tj returned an empty page. `in_force` rests on the MFA page as read today (undated).
- **tw-matsu** — NIA carriers' guide (edition of 2020-04-28): from 2015-01-01 a landing permit for Mainland Chinese entering "Kinmen, Matsu or Penghu" for travel (`own_rule = list`). 15 days from a Xinhua item on the PRC embassy site. `unit_level = lower` follows the passage (a county of the nominal Fuchien Province); since that province is nominal the county may count as first-level in practice — owner's call. `in_force` is weak: Wikipedia says the NIA allows it, but also that Beijing only lets Fujian residents travel to Matsu and that travel "has also been suspended since early 2020". Applies to one nationality only.
- **tz-zanzibar** — Only the insurance requirement is established (Tanzanian embassy notice of 2024-08-20; Wikipedia for current status). It is a paid insurance cover — close to "a mere fee". The lead's immigration checks on arrival from the mainland were **not found** in any text read. `top_level` rests on "Semi-autonomous region of Tanzania"; Zanzibar stands above Tanzania's ordinary regions.
- **ye-socotra** — Koryo Tours guide (2026-07-08) only; no official source. Socotra permit is not valid for mainland Yemen. Contradictions with the lead: entry "currently runs mainly through Jeddah" (Abu Dhabi "previously"); and "the island is visited via organised tours" with the operator arranging the permit — likely fails the independent-visitor test. No start date.
- **md-transnistria** — Wikipedia only. Migration card, 45 days; Ukrainians aged 18–60 need MGB permission since 2023-04-01. The lead's 1992 was not found. `not_a_unit`: a breakaway state; the corresponding Moldovan unit (Left Bank of the Dniester) is only mentioned in a hatnote.

Rows that look out of scope or marginal: ru-former-regional-evisas (abolished), tz-zanzibar (insurance fee), ye-socotra (organised tours), ru-chukotka (host organization), es-ceuta (local border traffic; two towns), in-minicoy-maldivians / az-nakhchivan / tw-matsu (one nationality), fr-clipperton (uninhabited), mx-southern-border-tvr (multi-state zone, one group of nationalities).
